Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I choose the right pouch for my setup?

Start with what you carry and how you use it. Match the pouch to your gear's size, decide how quickly you need to access it, and pick a mounting position you can reach without looking. A pouch that suits your discipline will usually speed up your handling.

2. What's the difference between nylon, Kydex and leather?

Nylon is light, flexible and easy to maintain. Kydex is rigid and holds its shape, which makes gear easy to re-holster or re-seat. Leather molds to your gear over time and looks classic, but it needs more care. The right choice depends on your use, budget and climate.

3. Does the mounting position really affect speed?

Yes. A pouch that sits in the same place every time lets you build muscle memory, so your hand finds the gear without searching. Small changes in angle or height can noticeably change how smoothly you reach it.

4. What should I pack for a multi-day mountaineering trip?

Pack around safety first: navigation, shelter, layers for changing weather, food and water, and emergency essentials. Then add only what supports your route. Weigh each item against how likely you are to need it, and plan your route before you decide what stays home.

5. How do I keep my pack light without giving up safety?

Choose multi-purpose items, skip duplicates, and never cut critical safety gear to save weight. Lay everything out before you leave and remove whatever you can't justify. A lighter, well-organized pack is easier to carry and safer on a long day.
